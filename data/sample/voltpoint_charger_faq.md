# VoltPoint Home Wallbox — Product FAQ

*Fictional sample product of Nordwind Mobility GmbH, for the RAG demo.*

## Technical specifications

The VoltPoint Home charges with up to 11 kW (three-phase, 16 A). The VoltPoint Home Pro variant supports up to 22 kW (three-phase, 32 A). Both models ship with a fixed 7.5 m Type 2 cable, are rated IP55 for outdoor installation and operate between -25 °C and +50 °C.

## Installation

The wallbox must be installed by a certified electrician. It requires a dedicated circuit protected by a type A residual-current device (RCD); 6 mA DC fault detection is built into the wallbox. In Germany, chargers up to 11 kW must be registered with the local grid operator, while chargers above 11 kW need the grid operator's approval before installation.

## Warranty

The standard warranty is 3 years from the date of purchase. It is extended to 5 years free of charge if the wallbox is registered in the VoltPoint app within 30 days of installation. The warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect installation.

## VoltPoint app

The VoltPoint app supports scheduled charging, solar surplus charging with a compatible PV inverter, monthly energy reports and management of RFID charging cards. The app is available for iOS and Android.

## Error codes and LED status

A blue LED means ready, a pulsing green LED means charging and a red LED indicates an error.

- E01: No protective earth (ground) connection detected. Stop using the charger and contact an electrician.
- E02: Overcurrent. The vehicle drew more current than allowed; charging stops and restarts after 60 seconds.
- E03: Overheating. The charger reduces charging power automatically and resumes full power once the internal temperature drops below 60 °C.
- E04: Communication error with the vehicle. Unplug the cable and plug it in again.
- E05: Residual-current or DC fault detected. Do not reset the device; contact an electrician.
