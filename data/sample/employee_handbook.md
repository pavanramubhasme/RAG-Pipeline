# Employee Handbook — Nordwind Mobility GmbH

*Fictional sample data for the RAG demo. Nordwind Mobility GmbH is not a real company.*

## Working hours

The regular working time for full-time employees is 40 hours per week. Nordwind uses flexitime: core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Thursday, and 10:00 to 13:00 on Fridays. Outside core hours employees organise their time freely, as long as team meetings are attended. Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager and is compensated with time off.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their probation period. Working from another EU country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year with manager approval. Remote work from outside the EU is not permitted for tax and data-protection reasons. Requests are submitted in the HR portal PeopleHub at least two weeks in advance.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year and must be taken by 31 March; days not taken by then expire. Vacation longer than two consecutive weeks must be requested at least four weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, notify your manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's certificate (AU-Bescheinigung) is required from the third calendar day of illness. Certificates are transmitted electronically by the doctor; employees only need to confirm the dates in PeopleHub.

## Business travel and expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip using the expense tool Spendly, with digital receipts attached. The meal allowance for domestic business trips is EUR 28 per full day and EUR 14 for arrival and departure days. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to EUR 150 per night, or EUR 200 per night in Munich and Frankfurt. Flights are booked in economy class. Train travel is second class; first class is allowed for journeys longer than four hours.

## Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of EUR 1,500 for courses, certifications and conferences. Spending requires manager approval in PeopleHub before booking. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year. In addition, employees may use up to five working days per year as paid learning days.
